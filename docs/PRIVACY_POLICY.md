# LinkUp Privacy Policy

*Effective: [publication date]. Applies to LinkUp for Android, version 1.0 and later.*

LinkUp lets phones on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot chat and share files directly with each other. It works without the internet, and it has no accounts and no servers.

## What LinkUp stores

On your phone only, in the app's private storage:

- **Your display name** and a random device ID that LinkUp creates on first start. They identify your phone to other phones on the same network.
- **Your chats and the files** you send and receive.
- **Settings**, such as theme and how you connect.
- **A short technical log** (connection events, errors), kept on the phone and never sent anywhere. It contains no message text and no file names.

LinkUp does not collect your phone number, contacts, location, photos you did not choose to send, advertising IDs or usage analytics.

## What other people see

Phones running LinkUp on the same local network can see your display name, your device ID, your phone's local network address and the app version. People you send messages or files to receive them on their phone, where they are stored by their copy of LinkUp.

## What leaves your phone

Only what you send, and only to the phones you send it to, over your local network. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party, and LinkUp never connects to the internet. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries.

**Messages and files are not encrypted on the local network in version 1.0.** Someone else on the same Wi-Fi with special tools could read them. Use LinkUp on networks you trust, or on your own hotspot.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to tell you about new messages and file progress.
- **Network access, Wi-Fi state, multicast:** to find and talk to phones on the same network. Android requires the "internet" permission for any network connection, even a local one; LinkUp does not use the internet.
- **Foreground service (connected device):** keeps transfers running, and keeps you reachable when you turn on *Stay reachable*, always with a visible notification.
- **Install apps:** only when you choose to open an app (APK) someone sent you; Android asks you first.
- **Storage (Android 8–9 only):** to save a file to Downloads when you ask.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Storage → *Clear all local data* removes your chats, files and name from the phone. Uninstalling LinkUp removes everything it stored. Copies already received by other people stay on their phones.

## Children

LinkUp is not directed at children under 13.
